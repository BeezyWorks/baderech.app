Privacy policy

שמירת הלשון, וגם שמירת המידע

The short version: no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold, ever. You don't need an account to learn or daven. If you make one, I keep what's needed to run it: your name, your email, your settings, your learning progress, bookmarks and notes, and the campaigns and events you create or join, so they follow you between devices. You can delete your account and all of that any time.

This policy covers baderech.app, Baderech accounts (auth.baderech.app), and the apps Mikros BaDerech, Mishnayos and Siddur One, plus any future Baderech apps, on the web, iOS and Android. They're built and run by Mattaniah Beezy (BeezyWorks), who is the data controller. "I" and "me" below means me. See also the terms of service.

What I collect

Your account

  • Email address, to sign you in and to send account and service emails, like verifying your address, resetting your password, or notices about a campaign you're part of. I don't send newsletters or marketing.
  • Name: your first name, last name and an optional display name, as you enter them or as Google or Apple share them when you sign in that way.
  • Password, if you use one. It's stored only as a one-way hash, so I can't read it.
  • Sessions: while you're signed in, I keep a session record with the IP address and browser or device type it came from, so you stay signed in and so abuse can be spotted. Sessions expire, and they're deleted when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Rate limiting: to stop people from guessing passwords, sign-in attempts are counted per IP address for a short time.

Signing in with Google or Apple

Google: I ask Google only for the basic sign-in permissions (openid, email and profile). From those I store your Google account ID, email address, whether Google has verified it, your name and your profile photo link, along with the sign-in tokens Google issues. I don't ask for or get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar or anything else in your Google account. I use this information only to sign you in and show who you are in the apps. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. I don't sell it, use it for ads, share it with anyone except as described here, or use it to train AI models.

Apple: I store your Apple account ID, your email address and, the first time you sign in, the name you choose to share, along with the tokens Apple issues. If you use "Hide My Email", I only ever see the relay address. When you delete your account, I revoke the app's access with Apple.

You can disconnect Google or Apple from your account, or revoke access from your Google or Apple account settings, at any time.

What your account syncs

When you're signed in, these are saved with your account so they're the same on all your devices:

  • App settings, like your nusach, language and display preferences in Siddur One. If you choose a location for zmanim as a setting, the place you chose is saved with it.
  • Learning progress: where you are, what you've finished, and streaks.
  • Bookmarks and notes you make on the texts. Your notes are private to you. Nobody else sees them, and I don't look at them unless you ask me to help with a problem.
  • Campaigns you create or join, like Mishnayos and Tehillim campaigns for shloshim, a refuah shleima or a siyum: the campaign, the name it's for, the name you sign up with, and what you've taken on and finished.
  • Events you create, with the details you enter, like a name, a date and a description.

If I start syncing something new, I'll update this page first.

What other people can see

Campaigns and events are meant to be shared. Anyone with the link to a campaign or event can see what's in it, including the name it's for, the organizer's name, and the names participants sign up with. Enter only what you're comfortable sharing, and get permission before using someone else's name. Organizers can close their campaigns and events, and if someone reports one, I get the report by email so I can review it.

Without an account

You can use the apps without signing in. Then your settings, progress, notes and bookmarks stay on your device (or in your browser on the web). Siddur One can use your device's location, if you allow it, to work out zmanim for where you are. That happens on your device, and your device's location is never sent to me. Reminders are scheduled on your device too.

Hosting logs

Like every website, the servers that deliver the apps see your IP address and basic request details. My hosting provider handles that as part of running the service, and I don't use it to track you.

How I use it

Only to run the apps: to sign you in, sync your things, run campaigns and events, send the emails you'd expect, keep the service secure, and answer you when you write. That's it.

What I don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks or ad IDs.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts, no third-party cookies, and no tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • No selling, renting or trading your information, to anyone, for any reason.
  • No using your data to train AI models.

Who else handles data

A few services do the work behind the scenes. Each one gets only what it needs, and only to provide its service to me:

  • Cloudflare hosts the websites, the apps' servers and their databases.
  • Resend delivers emails, so it sees your email address and the message.
  • Google and Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play deliver the apps. Their own privacy policies cover what they collect.

I'd also disclose information if the law required it. If the apps ever change hands, this policy would continue to apply to your data.

These services run in the United States and elsewhere, so your data may be processed outside your country.

Cookies

When you sign in on the web, a first-party cookie keeps you signed in across baderech.app sites. That's the only kind of cookie I set, and it's never used for tracking.

How long I keep it

  • Account data and synced data: for as long as you have an account.
  • Sessions: until they expire or you sign out.
  • Sign-in attempt counts: minutes.
  • Campaigns and events: until the organizer or I delete them, or the account that created them is deleted.
  • After deletion: deleted data can stay in automatic database backups for up to 30 days before it's gone for good.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Baderech account, and the data that goes with it, at any time:

  • In the app: go to Settings, then Account, then Delete account.
  • By email: write to mattaniah@beezyworks.com from the email address on the account, with the subject "Delete my Baderech account". I'll confirm it's you and delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

This deletes your account, name, email, password, sessions, connected Google and Apple sign-ins, settings, learning progress, bookmarks, notes, and the campaigns and events you created. If you used Sign in with Apple, I also revoke the app's access with Apple. In campaigns that other people organized, your name is removed, and the perakim you finished may stay counted without your name. Data on your device stays there until you remove the app.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your name, settings, notes and sign-in methods in the app.
  • Get a copy of what I hold about you, or have it corrected, by emailing me.
  • Delete your account, as described above.
  • Disconnect Google or Apple, or revoke access from their settings.

Wherever you live, you have these rights. You don't need to cite a law to use them. I'll answer within 30 days.

Children

The apps are fine for kids to learn and daven with, and they don't need an account to do it. Accounts aren't meant for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has made one, email me and I'll delete it.

Security

Everything is sent over HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access to the databases is limited to me. No system is perfect. If something ever goes wrong that affects your data, I'll tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, I'll say so in the apps or on the site, and by email if you have an account.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or about anything else: Mattaniah Beezy (BeezyWorks), mattaniah@beezyworks.com.
