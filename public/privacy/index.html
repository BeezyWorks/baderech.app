Privacy policy

שמירת הלשון, וגם שמירת המידע

The short version: no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold, ever. If you make an account, I keep your email, your name and what's needed to keep you signed in. What you learn, your notes, bookmarks and location stay on your device. You can have your account deleted any time.

This policy covers baderech.app, Baderech accounts (auth.baderech.app), and the apps Mikros BaDerech, Mishnayos and Siddur One on the web, iOS and Android. They're built and run by Mattaniah Beezy (BeezyWorks). "I" and "me" below means me. See also the terms of service.

What I collect

If you make a Baderech account

  • Email address, to sign you in and to send account emails (verify your address, reset your password). I don't send newsletters or marketing.
  • Name: your first name, last name and an optional display name, as you enter them or as Google or Apple share them when you sign in that way.
  • Password, if you use one. It's stored only as a one-way hash, so I can't read it.
  • Google or Apple sign-in: if you connect one, I store the account ID they give me and the sign-in tokens they issue. I use those only to sign you in, and for Apple, to revoke access when you delete your account. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", I only ever see the relay address.
  • Sessions: when you're signed in, I keep a session record with the IP address and browser or device type it came from, so you stay signed in and so abuse can be spotted. Sessions expire, and they're deleted when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Rate limiting: to stop people from guessing passwords, sign-in attempts are counted per IP address for a short time.

When you use the apps

Your learning progress, streaks, notes, bookmarks and settings are stored on your device (or in your browser on the web). Siddur One uses your location, only if you allow it, to work out zmanim and the right davening for where you are. That calculation happens on your device, and your location is never sent to me. Reminders are scheduled on your device too.

If I ever add syncing between devices through your account, I'll update this page before it ships and tell you exactly what gets stored.

Shloshim and siyum campaigns (Mishnayos)

If you create or join a campaign, I store what you enter for it: the name of the person it's for, the organizer name, the names participants sign up with, and which perakim they took. Anyone with the campaign link can see these names, so enter only what you're comfortable sharing. If someone reports a campaign, I get the report by email so I can review it. Campaign organizers can close a campaign, and you can ask me to take one down.

Hosting logs

Like every website, the servers that deliver these pages see your IP address and basic request details. My hosting provider handles that as part of running the service, and I don't use it to track you.

What I don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks or ad IDs.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts, and no third-party cookies.
  • No selling, renting or trading your information, to anyone, for any reason.
  • No reading your notes. They're on your device, not mine.

Who else handles data

A few services do the work behind the scenes. Each one gets only what it needs:

  • Cloudflare hosts the websites, the account service and its database.
  • Resend delivers account emails, so it sees your email address and the message.
  • Google and Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play deliver the apps. Their own privacy policies cover what they collect.

Cookies

When you sign in on the web, a single first-party cookie keeps you signed in across baderech.app sites. That's the only cookie I set, and it's never used for tracking.

Your choices

  • Delete your account from the app, or by emailing me. That deletes your account, sessions and connected sign-ins right away. If you used Sign in with Apple, I also revoke the app's access with Apple.
  • Ask me anything, including for a copy of what I hold about you, by emailing mattaniah@beezyworks.com.

Wherever you live, you have these rights. You don't need to cite a law to use them.

Children

The apps are fine for kids to learn and daven with, and they don't need an account to do it. Accounts aren't meant for children under 13. If you think a child has made one, email me and I'll delete it.

Security

Everything is sent over HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access to the account database is limited to me. No system is perfect. If something ever goes wrong that affects your data, I'll tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, I'll say so on the site, and by email if you have an account.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or about anything else: mattaniah@beezyworks.com.
