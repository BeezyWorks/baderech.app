Torah for the way.

"When you walk on the way," it says in Shema. These are apps for that time: a perek of Mishnayos on the bus, shnayim mikra in the carpool line, and a siddur that already knows what day it is. They're free, they have no ads, and they get the details right.

Mikros BaDerech

מקראות בדרך
Live · web

Tanach with Targum and mefarshim, in the spirit of a Mikraos Gedolos you can carry in your pocket. Go by sefer and perek, or go through the parsha aliyah by aliyah and finish with the haftara for your minhag. Tap any pasuk to see Rashi and the rest. Built for shnayim mikra v'echad targum, wherever erev Shabbos finds you.

  • Shnayim mikra v'echad targum
  • Haftara by minhag
  • All of Tanach with mefarshim
  • Notes & bookmarks

Mishnayos

משניות
Coming to the stores soon

Shisha Sidrei Mishna with translation and the meforshim you'd want next to it: Bartenura, Rambam, Yachin and Ikar Tosafos Yom Tov. It remembers where you are, so you actually get to the hadran. Mishna Yomi is right on the home screen, and when someone needs a zechus you can split up Shas Mishnayos among friends, l'iluy nishmas or l'refuah shleima, and watch the perakim get taken.

  • Bartenura · Rambam · Yachin · Tosafos Yom Tov

  • Shloshim & siyum campaigns

Siddur One

סידור
Live · web & iOS

A siddur that knows what day it is. Rosh Chodesh, chol hamoed, a fast day, whether there's Tachanun: it shows you what you're actually saying today, in your nusach, so you aren't flipping pages hunting for Ya'aleh V'yavo. Zmanim for where you are, and it's on your Apple Watch too.

  • Ashkenaz · Sfard · Edot HaMizrach · Ari
  • Zmanim & home screen widget
  • Apple Watch
  • 9 languages

One account for all of them

A Baderech account works in every app here. You don't need one to learn or daven. Everything works without signing in. It's for when you want the apps to know who you are.

Make one with your email, Google or Apple. You can change your name, connect another sign-in, or delete the whole thing from this site whenever you want.
